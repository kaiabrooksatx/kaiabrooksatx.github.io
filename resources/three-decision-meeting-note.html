The 3-Decision Meeting Note

Most meeting summaries are too broad to use. This turns a transcript into the next three things that actually change what happens next.

Paste this into your tool of choice

Read the transcript below. Do not summarize the whole meeting.

Return exactly these sections:

## Decisions (maximum 3)
- [decision] — owner: [name or unassigned] — due: [date or unknown]

## Open loops (maximum 3)
- [what remains unresolved] — next action: [specific action]

## Receipts
- [short quote or timestamp supporting each decision]

Rules:
- A decision is something the group agreed to do, stop, choose, or defer.
- Do not infer commitments that are not stated.
- If no decision was made, say "No decision recorded" rather than inventing one.
- Preserve uncertainty: use "unknown" where the transcript does not name an owner or date.
