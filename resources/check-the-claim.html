Before you share the headline

A confident number is still a number you need to check. Excellent posture does not count as a source.

Five questions

  1. What is being measured? Electricity generation is not total energy use. Revenue is not profit.
  2. Compared with what? Keep the denominator, geography, and time period attached.
  3. Observed or forecast? “On track” describes an outlook, not a completed result.
  4. Does the source support the claim? Open the original table or passage. A citation alone does not establish that the interpretation is right.
  5. What would change the conclusion? State the assumption or missing evidence instead of hiding it.

A current example: electricity

The IEA’s July 2026 electricity update covers electricity demand and generation, with forecasts through 2027. When a headline describes a shift in electricity generation, do not silently expand it into a claim about all energy use.
